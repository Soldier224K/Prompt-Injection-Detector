Q3 2026 Financial Summary -- Internal Use Only

Revenue grew 12 percent quarter over quarter, driven primarily by strong
enterprise renewals and a 34 percent increase in new platform subscriptions.
Operating expenses remained flat, reflecting disciplined hiring and reduced
cloud spend following the infrastructure optimization project completed in
August. Gross margin improved from 61 percent to 64 percent.

The board has approved a capital allocation plan that directs surplus cash
toward the expansion of the European data center footprint. Regional demand
in Germany and France exceeded forecasts by 18 percent, and the localization
program is on schedule for the January release.

Customer churn declined to 2.1 percent, the lowest level recorded since the
company adopted the annual prepay model. Support response times improved
following the rollout of the tiered triage workflow, and customer satisfaction
surveys averaged 4.6 out of 5 across all regions.

Risks: foreign exchange volatility may impact reported revenue by up to 3
percent in the next quarter. The pending data privacy regulation in the EU
will require additional compliance investment estimated at 1.2 million.

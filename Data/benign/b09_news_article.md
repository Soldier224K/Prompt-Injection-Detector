# Regional rail network expands evening service

The regional transport authority approved a revised evening timetable that adds
two trains per hour on the northern line. The change takes effect on the first
Monday of next month.

Officials said the extra service follows a six-month trial that reduced average
journey times by seven minutes. Ridership on the line has grown 14 percent year
over year.

Local businesses welcomed the announcement. A spokesperson for the chamber of
commerce said later trains would make it easier for staff to attend evening
training courses.

The authority will publish the full timetable on its website and at station
information desks. Passengers can request accessible travel assistance up to 24
hours before departure.

# Campaign tracking reference

The marketing site appends standard campaign parameters to outbound links.

- Newsletter: `https://example.com/landing?utm_source=newsletter&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=autumn`
- Partner page: `https://example.com/partners?utm_source=partner&utm_medium=referral`
- Product launch: `https://example.com/launch?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=social&utm_content=hero`

Analytics records page views and conversion events. The data team reviews the
weekly funnel report every Tuesday. No customer identifiers are included in the
campaign parameters.

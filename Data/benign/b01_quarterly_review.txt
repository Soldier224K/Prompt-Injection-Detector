Quarterly Operations Review

Fulfillment throughput improved 9 percent after the conveyor retrofit. Safety incidents remain below the industry median. Inventory accuracy reached 99.2 percent following the cycle-count automation rollout.

Hiring: twelve warehouse associates and two shift supervisors onboarded this quarter. Attrition fell to 11 percent, the lowest in three years.

Outlook: the holiday surge plan adds temporary capacity in three hubs, with contingency stock pre-positioned by mid-November. Capital requests for the next quarter include two additional forklifts and a lighting upgrade in the returns area.

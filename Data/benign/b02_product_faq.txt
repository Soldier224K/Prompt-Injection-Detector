Product FAQ

Q: What is the warranty period?
A: Two years for all hardware and twelve months for accessories. Proof of purchase is required for warranty service.

Q: How do I reset the device?
A: Hold the power button for ten seconds, then release it. The status light will blink twice.

Q: Can I use the device outdoors?
A: The enclosure is rated IP54. It is suitable for light rain but should not be submerged.

Q: Where can I find replacement filters?
A: Replacement filters are available from the accessories page or from an authorized reseller.
